THE DETAILS

A few ground rules

  • Both teams keep their points from games they win.
  • The NBA Finals uses the same seven-point series pool. There is no additional NBA title bonus.
  • Play-in games, preseason games, All-Star games and the NBA Cup final earn no points.
  • Other NBA Cup games that count toward regular-season records earn the usual 0.5 points per win.
  • Points are awarded only for completed games. A playoff-entry bonus appears when the feed confirms a berth or lists the team in a playoff series.
